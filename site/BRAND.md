# agent-box brand

agent-box's identity. It lives in the same warm, crafted family as Murmur (blue + cream, soft light,
editorial type), because that is the house look these projects share — but agent-box has its own
hero, its own signature element and its own voice. It should feel like a sibling, never a copy.

## Idea

On-demand compute carved out of a gaming PC. Warm and reassuring, not cold or industrial: the PC is
a friendly machine lending its spare cores, and it always hands them back when a game wants them.
The feeling is "a tidy workshop", not "a server rack".

## Color

Four colors and their mixes. Blue and cream carry the page; butter is the one loud accent; ink is
the text. Nothing else.

| Token      | Hex       | Use |
|------------|-----------|-----|
| `--blue`   | `#225BFF` | the ground: hero, logo tile, primary surfaces |
| `--cream`  | `#FFFBEA` | the sheet, text on blue |
| `--butter` | `#FDEE8C` | the one accent: headline highlight, the "full power" state |
| `--ink`    | `#0E1B45` | text on cream, the terminal |

Tints and shades are mixed from these four, so no other hue creeps in. On the status card the three
modes read in meaning: My PC is quiet ink, Shared is blue, Full power is butter.

## Type

Inter throughout (400–800), the same editorial sans the house look uses, with a monospace
(`ui-monospace`) for commands and small labels. Big, tight headlines; calm body.

## Logo

A box drawn as a cream wireframe cube on a blue rounded tile — a container, and the unit of compute
agent-box hands out. It carries the brand on its own as the favicon and OG card. (Kept from the
first cut, which Tyler liked.)

## What makes it *not* a Murmur clone

- No reeded / fluted glass hero, no refraction filter, no floating agent-logo chips, no ghost.
- agent-box's hero is a **stacked-box composition**: the cube mark, a soft blue→butter light, and a
  live **status card** — the PC's mode and the boxes running on it — as the signature element,
  because the one thing agent-box does that nothing else does is hand out and reclaim compute.
- Its own section rhythm: a stat strip, a three-step "one command" flow, a parts grid, and a mode
  table, each with its own shape, rather than Murmur's page-by-page scroll of set pieces.

## Motifs

- The cube, repeated small as a bullet/opener.
- A soft radial glow (blue core, butter rim) behind the hero, instead of fluted glass.
- The status card: rounded cream panel on blue, with a mode dial and stacked box rows.
- Rounded, friendly geometry (12–20px radii); generous whitespace; one butter accent per view.
