# Project Fano brand

Project Fano (`fano.afig.dev`) is named for Robert Fano (1917–2016), who founded MIT's Project MAC in 1963 and pioneered time-sharing: turning one computer into a shared compute utility.

It shares the a-Fig house palette with Murmur, with its own mark and a simpler, static layout.

## Colors

Four colors and their mixes. Nothing else.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--blue` | `#225BFF` | Hero ground, icon tile, links on cream |
| `--cream` | `#FFFBEA` | Sheet background, primary text on blue, mascot faces |
| `--butter` | `#FDEE8C` | Single accent: hero highlight, primary button, mascot flank |
| `--ink` | `#0E1B45` | Text and rules on cream |

## Mark

The rounded cube mascot (`site/favicon.svg`): cream top and front faces with two pill-shaped cutouts, and a butter right flank, set bare on cobalt or inside a rounded cobalt tile (`rx="15"` on a `64×64` viewBox).

## Type and surfaces

- **Inter** (400–800) for headings and body; system monospace (`ui-monospace`) for commands and short status labels.
- Subtle vertical grid lines and faint paper grain over the cobalt hero, meeting a rounded cream sheet with a `2px` ink top rule and hairline rows.

## Voice

Plain sentences for peers. Say what the software does; cut narration, filler, and anything implied.
